==============================================================
== egress policy: an isolated network namespace
==============================================================
the sandbox gets its own netns and the pinned slirp4netns datapath; the host
reaches opencode through an explicit port forward, and the host's loopback is
closed on both routes (127.0.0.1 inside the namespace and slirp's 10.0.2.2).
