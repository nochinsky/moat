==============================================================
== egress policy: an isolated network namespace
==============================================================
the sandbox gets its own netns and the pinned slirp4netns datapath, with nothing
forwarded in: the host's loopback is closed on both routes (127.0.0.1 inside the
namespace and slirp's 10.0.2.2 gateway), and the box has no host-facing server.
